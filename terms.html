02The most important clause here. What you are buying, and what you are not.

What TalkPat is

TalkPat is a practice tool for your own communication skills. Through short micro-reflections and open conversations it helps you gather and organise context about how you communicate with your children — across different areas and situations — and then puts that context in front of a general-purpose AI model so the response speaks to your patterns rather than a generic case.

Organising that context is the entire value we offer, and we would rather be precise than oversell:

  • We add no proprietary counsel. There is no TalkPat method, no in-house parenting framework, no clinical protocol, and no editorial layer of our own over the model's output.
  • No professional reviews anything. No psychologist, therapist, educator, or family counsellor reads your reflections, checks the replies, or stands behind them. There is no human in the loop.
  • Replies are machine-generated. They are produced statistically by OpenAI's model and can be confidently wrong, out of date, inconsistent, or invented. Better context improves relevance; it does not make output correct.

What the assistant is scoped to refuse

The assistant is deliberately limited to your communication skills. It is designed to decline, and you should not rely on it if it fails to decline, requests to:

  • assess, diagnose, screen, or interpret your child's behaviour, development, personality, or mental health;
  • evaluate whether your child has any condition, or what stage or level they are at;
  • give clinical, medical, psychological, educational, or legal advice about your child or your family;
  • produce anything resembling a report, assessment, opinion, or care plan.

These limits are the product working as intended, not a defect. If a reply strays across one of them anyway — models are imperfect and this can happen — treat it as unreliable output rather than as guidance, and please tell us so we can tighten the scope.

TalkPat is a place to think about how you speak to your family. It is not a substitute for a paediatrician, a mental health professional, a teacher, or a family therapist, and you should not delay seeking any of them because of something you read here. Every decision about your child remains yours, and yours to answer for.

03When to close the app and call someone.

Urgent situations

TalkPat is not an emergency service and cannot get help to you. Nobody is monitoring what you write. A reflection typed at 3am reaches no one.

If a child is in immediate danger, if anyone is at risk of serious harm, or if you are worried about your own safety, contact your local emergency number — 112 across the European Union — or go to an emergency department.

If you are struggling with your own mental health, or having thoughts of harming yourself or anyone else, please reach out to a doctor, a crisis line, or someone you trust today. Those feelings are more common among parents than most people admit, and real help exists. TalkPat is not it.

Do not use TalkPat to work through a medical emergency, a suspected injury, or an acute mental health crisis. Where a reply suggests contacting a professional, treat that as the only part worth acting on immediately.
