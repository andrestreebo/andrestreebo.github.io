In plain words

TalkPat works on your communication skills as a parent. The subject of every reflection is you — how you spoke, what you noticed, what you want to try differently. Not your child.

Your reflections are stored [[STORAGE]]. We run no servers that hold them. When you send a message, the relevant part of your reflections goes to OpenAI's API so a reply can be generated, and OpenAI deletes it within 30 days. It is never used to train a model. Nobody at TalkPat reads it.

There is no account, no email address, and no advertising anywhere in this app.

02What the app is, described honestly as a data flow.

How TalkPat works

TalkPat has two inputs. Micro-reflections are short prompts about your own communication — how a conversation went, where you got stuck, what you noticed in yourself. Open chats are free conversations on the same subject. Together they build up a picture of your communication habits across different areas, which is what lets the app respond to your actual patterns rather than generic advice.

That accumulated picture is the useful part of TalkPat and the reason it differs from typing a question into a general chatbot: you don't re-explain yourself every time. It is also a candid record of how you speak to your family, which is why it stays on your device.

Where it lives

[[STORAGE]]. TalkPat has no backend of its own — no user database, no server-side copy of your reflections, no admin panel through which anyone here could read them.

What is sent when you send a message

To generate a reply, TalkPat transmits to the OpenAI API the message you just wrote plus the portion of your reflections needed to make the reply relevant. This travels over an encrypted connection, is used only to produce that reply, and is covered by the retention and training terms in §05.

Nothing is sent anywhere else, and there is no step at which a human at TalkPat reviews it.

04The scope limit that matters most. Read this one if you read only one.

What TalkPat deliberately leaves out

TalkPat is a skills tool for the adult using it. Your child is not a subject of this app, and by design:

  • We collect nothing from your child. There is no child profile, no questionnaire about them, no tracking of their behaviour, and no way for a child to be enrolled, observed, or logged in.
  • Nothing comes back from your child. There is no feedback channel, no shared account, no report sent to them or about them, and no second side to any conversation here.
  • Nothing is assessed. The assistant is scoped to your communication skills and declines requests to evaluate, diagnose, screen, or interpret your child's behaviour or development. See §02 of the Terms.
  • Nothing profiles anyone. No score, ranking, risk flag, or assessment of you or your family is produced, stored, or shared. There is no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

Because you will naturally describe real conversations, your child may be mentioned in your own words. Keep that light — an initial or "my youngest" works as well as a name, and TalkPat never asks for a name, age, school, clinic, or diagnosis. The prompts are about your side of the exchange.

We do not report anything you write to any authority, school, clinic, employer, or insurer. We could not: we do not hold it.

05The one company that sees your words. Exactly what it may do with them.

OpenAI: training and retention

Replies are generated by OpenAI, L.L.C. through its API, under a data processing agreement that appoints OpenAI as our processor. Two commitments matter, and both come from OpenAI's published enterprise terms rather than from us:

Not used for training

Content submitted through the OpenAI API is not used to train or improve OpenAI's models. This is the default for API traffic and differs from the consumer ChatGPT product, which is not involved here.

Deleted within 30 days

OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days in order to provide the service and detect abuse, after which they are removed from its systems, unless it is legally required to keep them for longer. [[ZDR_NOTE]]

Within that window, access is restricted to a limited number of authorised OpenAI personnel and only for the purposes of abuse investigation and legal compliance. Nobody at TalkPat can see it at any point.

Further detail: openai.com/enterprise-privacy and OpenAI's data controls documentation.

10What protects it, and what doesn't.

Security

Traffic between TalkPat and the services above is encrypted in transit with TLS. Your reflections sit in the operating system's app-private storage, so other apps cannot read them. Because we run no servers and hold no user database, there is no central store of anyone's reflections to be breached.

The realistic risk is therefore the device itself. A passcode or biometric lock is the single most useful thing you can do to protect what you write here.

No system is perfectly secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
