03Where the numbers come from, and what they are worth.

Prices are indicative and cached

The app says so on every screen that shows one, and it means it: "Indicative prices from cached searches, shown in your currency. They are not live fares and can change before you book."

The figures come from searches other travellers ran recently, collected through our data partner and stored so that a comparison across dozens of destinations is possible at all — a live search across every candidate city for five travellers would take minutes and would not be a usable app. The trade-off is that a price can be stale, sold out, or simply gone.

We try to be straight with you about this rather than bury it:

  • Every result carries the age of its price, in plain words such as "updated 7h ago".
  • Results are flagged when any price in the set is more than two days old.
  • Prices older than three days are excluded from the ranking altogether.
  • Where a departure city has no cached prices at all, the app tells you so instead of returning an empty screen.

Fares are also converted into your chosen currency using recent reference rates, so what you see may differ from what your card is finally charged. Treat every number as a starting point for a decision, and verify before you commit money.

04What happens when you tap through, and how we get paid.

Booking, and our commission

When you choose to check live fares, we open your browser at our travel partner's site with the route and dates filled in. Everything after that — availability, the real price, baggage, changes, cancellation, refunds and any dispute — is governed by that site's terms and the airline's, not ours.

The link identifies ManyToFly as the referrer, and if you book we may earn a commission. We think you should know two things about that. It costs you nothing: the price is the same as going there directly. And it does not buy position: destinations are ranked by what the group pays, never by what we earn, and no airline or agency can pay to appear.
